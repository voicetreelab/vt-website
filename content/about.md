Hi I'm Manu, a software engineer from Sydney. I built Voicetree because I was frustrated at work losing track of what I was meant to be doing and defaulting to slack.

Managing context across multiple tasks and AI coding agents was hard. I wanted to think out loud and have my thoughts become a tree structure I could actually work within.

I am now filled with purpose to build my vision of the world where our brains are augmented by AI to fill in our cognitive gaps and multiply our strengths. I think the worlds hardest problems aren't solved not because for a lack of resources but for a lack of having found the right solution.

Voicetree is in early alpha. I'm building in public and would love your feedback.
