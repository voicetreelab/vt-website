<div class="faq-section">
<p class="section-label faq-title">Frequently Asked Questions</p>
<div class="faq-list">
<details class="faq-item" open>
<summary class="faq-question">Will this have access to my data?</summary>
<div class="faq-answer">Voicetree is local-first: your files stay only on your machine as plain Markdown. When you use AI features, content is sent to third-party APIs (Google, Soniox) for processing &mdash; these providers do not store your data for training. We collect anonymous usage telemetry. See our privacy policy for details.</div>
</details>
<details class="faq-item">
<summary class="faq-question">Is this an Obsidian plugin?</summary>
<div class="faq-answer">It started as one (agent-canvas on GitHub), but outgrew the plugin ecosystem. Voicetree is now a standalone Electron app that opens your existing Obsidian vaults. Your files stay as plain Markdown.</div>
</details>
<details class="faq-item">
<summary class="faq-question">Can I use it without AI features?</summary>
<div class="faq-answer">Yes. AI is completely optional and off by default. Use Voicetree as a pure spatial markdown editor &mdash; AI won't see or modify your notes unless you explicitly launch an agent.</div>
</details>
<details class="faq-item">
<summary class="faq-question">Can I keep my current agent workflows?</summary>
<div class="faq-answer">Yes. Voicetree runs the same agents you already have installed on your computer in embedded terminals. You can use them exactly as you're already using them.</div>
</details>
